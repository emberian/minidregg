A Refusal Explains Itself
When a transition is rejected, the kernel returns the specific law clause or capability check that failed along with the values it evaluated.
For example, house-rules grants public observation but restricts mutation to its owner. Submitting an append without a mutation grant fails with a no-grant refusal.
why <attempt-id>
can --any <ref>
The why verb decodes the retained refusal frame in the kernel's terms, while can --any dry-runs the resource law to find an operation your current grants cover.
Refusals are retained in workspace history alongside admitted turns. By contrast, field-notes carries a mutation grant for sage.
