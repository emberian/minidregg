Welcome to Mini
Mini is a shared workspace where documents, rooms, task boards, and Sandstorm applications live under explicit capability grants and machine-checked rules.
what-is-mini covers the architecture: proposals, the Lean admission kernel, and world-root receipts.
a-refusal-explains-itself shows how rejected writes return the exact rule clause that stopped them, and how can --any derives an admissible request.
the-lost-reply explains how undecided network calls are retained locally so retries never double-apply or double-charge.
house-rules is read-only for most accounts; attempts to append without a mutation grant are refused.
field-notes is open to delegated collaborators under a bounded per-turn cost ceiling.
the-lamplighters-note introduces tale, a text adventure governed by a sealed transition law.
Shared rooms include lobby (general conversation with the Hermes librarian resident) and operations (build, checkpoint, and backup logs).
