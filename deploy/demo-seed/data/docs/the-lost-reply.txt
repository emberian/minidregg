The Lost Reply
When a connection drops after a request is sent, the client cannot tell from silence alone whether the kernel admitted the turn.
Before transmitting any proposal, the workspace seals the exact request bytes to disk as a durable attempt record.
lookup <attempt-id>  |  retry <attempt-id>
Retrying an undecided attempt resends those exact bytes under the same attempt identifier. The kernel deduplicates by attempt, so the transition executes at most once and charges at most once.
