The Lamplighter's Note
At the end of the lane stands a narrow house where an upstairs lamp has hummed for forty years.
"The lamp wants a key, and the key wants a door."
North of the hallway, beyond the anteroom, a locked door waits for whoever holds the brass key.
The house is modeled as the interactive story tale, governed by a sealed room law checked by the Lean theorem sealed_story_scene_monotone.
