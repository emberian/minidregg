# The hallway

A hallway. A door north.

The floorboards are the colour of weak tea and they remember every
foot that has crossed them; they remember yours now too. Behind you
there is only the way you came in, and it has quietly stopped being a
way. Ahead, the door stands a hand's width open, and through the gap
comes lamplight and the smell of cold iron.
