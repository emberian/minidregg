# The lamp room

The key turns as if it has been oiled this morning. Inside: a round
room, windows all the way around, and in the middle a great lamp,
unlit. There is a box of matches on the sill. There is a logbook open
beside it, and every page is signed.

Whoever lights the lamp is the lamplighter now. Write your name under
the others'.

(This is the house's other ending. Everything you did to get here is in
its history, and the house does not forget.)
