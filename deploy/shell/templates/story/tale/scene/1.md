# The anteroom

A small square room, lit by one lamp that nobody seems to tend. On a
nail beside the lamp hangs a key: long, black, heavier than it looks.
It is the only thing in the house that appears to be waiting for
someone.

To the west, a glass door fogged from the other side. To the north, a
narrow stair climbs into the dark.

    take key   the key on the nail
    go 2       west, through the glass door
    go 3       north, up the stair
