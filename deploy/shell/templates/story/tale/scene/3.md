# The landing

The stair ends at a landing barely wide enough to stand on, and at an
iron door. It is not ornate. It has one keyhole, set at the height of a
hand that is used to turning keys, and a latch that does not move.

Behind it something hums, low and patient, like a kettle that has been
thinking about boiling for a hundred years.

    go 4    through the iron door, if you have its key
