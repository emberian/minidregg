# The glasshouse

Warm air, and green. Somebody planted this room a long time ago and
then let it keep its own counsel: vines over the panes, a bench worn
smooth, a tap that drips in no hurry. The glass door has closed behind
you, and from this side it has no handle.

It is a good place to stop. Sit on the bench. Say something to whoever
else is in the house; the walls here carry voices well.

(There is no way on from here. This is one of the house's endings.)
