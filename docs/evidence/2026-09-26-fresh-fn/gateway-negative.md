# Signed gateway refusal probe

The source-owned [native probe](../../../scripts/fn-e1e2/probe_gateway_direct_submit.lean) passed against a fresh B Mini Store on September 26. The Store held a gateway resource at target 600 with a current law requiring request subject 7. Ordinary subject 8 held a valid delegated observe/mutate child grant 63 for that same target. The probe used its private key to sign a real `DeclaredResourceController.Command`, called `NativeHost.submitLoaded`, and required the receiver's specific `invoke/policyRejected` result. It then called the public `NativeHost.submit` path and required uniform `admission/request refused`.

The probe compared the **full canonical durable image bytes** before and after both refusals using decidable `ByteArray` equality; accepted-count equality alone was not counted as sufficient. [The captured log](gateway-negative.log) records exit 0 and the pass verdict. The exact signed call remains in the private fixture at `/tmp/mini-fn-setup-probe-parent/gateway-pinned-b/gateway-direct-submit-probe.M0FAUR/direct-call.bin` (1,090 bytes, SHA-256 `684f5a606d44450ba24801a1fa2815da1d1a0079a709f43711f0983509f256f4`); custody keys and Store bytes are not copied here.

This is a receiver-level negative under a **valid ordinary target mutation grant**, not merely absence of capability or refusal by the public observation planner. It establishes the gateway subject law on this fixture. It does not establish fn Store provenance or large V2 payload admission.
