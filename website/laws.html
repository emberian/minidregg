Laws and objects

A law is a predicate the kernel checks on every change to a resource, whoever proposed it. Objects, activities and seats build on that rule. All of what follows is compiled and proved in Lean; none of it is reachable from a Host operation yet unless a row says so.

Three things called law

  • A cell law is a decidable predicate over a write's old and new state and its request. It is the judge of admission.
  • A law in .obend is a property of a specification's methods, kept as a closure in its metadata. Nothing discharges it and it never reaches admission.
  • requires names a member a specification needs from its final self. It is not a precondition, and it is recorded, not checked.

Rules for admission written in .obend (invariant, guard, permit, forbid) are a design, not built: a program cannot yet state the law of its own object.

Which code wrote

Every law state begins with the slot objective/artifact. For a command with an Objective claim it holds the claimed method's identity; for any other command it holds -1. A clause objectivePin accepts only steps whose slot names a pinned artifact, so an ordinary command is refused whatever its writes. No turn installs that clause at object birth yet.

Objects

A cell is an object to the kernel only if it has a record: its package pin, its law, an upgrade policy and a payer. A birth on a cell without a record is refused, and so is a birth of any package but the pinned one. Every write to an object's declared state, from a birth, a resume or a direct write, is judged by the object's law over the old and new state. A resumed activity's write is judged as its birth subject, never as whoever delivered the response. A refusal names the failing clause and commits nothing.

Activities

An activity is a persisted checkpoint plus one await. Its reply slot has one decider and one deadline, decided once. A resume delivers the settled outcome together with the object's state and version read in the same turn, so no write is computed from a stale read. An await that exhausts its declared envelope is a paid turn; an ended activity leaves an empty tombstone; anyone may abandon an await after its deadline plus a grace, which returns the purse minus the timeout fee. One public tariff prices every declared envelope, for native admission and for activities.

Seats

A seat holds one side of a contract in its own Book account. Its law is offer safety: every want is met, or every give is refunded. The law is judged on every reallocation, and the offerer's exit is a kernel action no contract clause is consulted for.
