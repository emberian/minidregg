Origin

Why minidregg exists

A multi-million-line formal stack already proved the derivation machine. Almost none of it was on the load path. minidregg is the inversion.

The numbers

Corpus Scale Note
breadstuffs total ~2.89M LOC 1.82M Rust · 913K Lean · ~160K TS/JS
minidregg budget ≤10% (~290K) analysis says we need far less
semantic core (prior) ~25K the meaning was small; the twins were not

Central diagnosis

Sound lowerings. Parallel twins. Empty load path.

Breadstuffs built and proved every piece of the derivation machine, demonstrated each at n=1, and wired almost none of them into the live path. The distance to “write once, derive executor + proofs + circuits” was never mathematical — every lowering is sound. The mass exists because each generic mechanism landed beside the hand-written corpus it should have replaced.

  • eDSL → VCG → whole-run safety — complete vertical slice, used once (its own demo). Dozens of apps hand-written beside it.
  • Parametric app toolkit — proved once; almost never adopted. Historical twins persist gated and ungated.
  • Logic→IR compiler — real and sound; reachability is not authorship.
  • Kernel minimality theorems — three verbs over a proved guard lattice. Never executed.

The corpus contained its own refactor plan, proved as theorems, and did not execute it. Additive-extension culture. No delete-the-twin discipline. Too many concurrent lanes in one tree.

Founding inversion

n=1 and n=all coincide by construction.

Minidregg’s founding move: the derived path is the only path. Nothing lands beside; everything replaces; delete the twin. Before writing any AIR, constraint, circuit, or executor arm by hand — stop. It comes out of the compiler, or the compiler grows the missing vocabulary.

Distilled thesis

A turn is the exercise of an attenuable, proof-carrying token over owned state, leaving a verifiable receipt.

Four substances

  • Value — linear (Σδ = 0 exactly)
  • Authority — generative under non-forgeability
  • Evidence — monotone
  • State — guarded-mutable (frame rule)

Authority = constructive knowledge

Proof-checking is cheap and trusted. Proof-search is undecidable and untrusted. To hold a capability is to be able to exhibit a witness that verifies.

One Pred algebra, four polarities

Caveat · program · precondition · intent demand. Two computed dials: coordination price and disclosure ladder.

Spine in five words

Determination is eager; witness is lazy. Every hole is a lazy witness over an eager shape. Never build a hole that defers its δ or its guard.

Why Loom (not one universal field)

No single arithmetic is honestly native to everything we need.

Bits, sparse memory, prime-field proofs, residue-ring FHE, MPC shares, privacy, and unbounded causal history do not share one honest native dialect. Forcing them into one universal encoding hides conversions and usually moves the hardest security claims into handwritten glue.

Loom owns the joints: roots-before-challenges control, explicit failure events and error budgets, exact common-opening relations, proof-carrying history attribution, and Lean-owned admission. Native dialects keep the work they do well.

This choice is falsifiable. If a simpler formalizable stack supplies the same heterogeneous guarantees — or if Loom’s joins do not remove duplication and benchmark competitively — we replace it.
