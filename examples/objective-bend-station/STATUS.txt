Station shared-consequence source — status rewritten 2026-10-04

Despite the directory name, every .bend file here is upstream-Bend source checked
through the retiring BendTT path (sealed upstream parser/emitter, BendTT
Book.check). None of it is Objective Bend Core4, and none of it runs on the Core4
demand machine. What survives is the design: one global property owner, a shared
oxygen supply, take/drop/move as ordered writes to the player and the shared
resource together, refusal as a value rather than an empty Plan, and adoption of
changed rules as an authorized step separate from installation.

To run on Core4 the station must be ported to .obend. SharedConsequences and
EngineeringStation are the first candidates. The port needs constructs Core4 lacks
today (sums with case for the refusal/Plan result; lists for inventories); see
docs/OBJECTIVE-BEND.md.

Historical (2026-10-03, BendTT path): SharedConsequences, EngineeringStation and
the synthetic Receiving Books passed BendTT Book.check (117/123/134 definitions);
the NativePlans/NativeMethods/NativeReceiving manifests passed the sealed source
checker; the bounded scripted resident provider passed six boundary tests. No
native installation, hosted GM, deployment or paid-model claim was ever made.
The BendTT receiving test (testing/StationSourcePlanReceiving.lean) depends on
Compiler.BendScalarPlanAdapter, Compiler.BendCoreAdmission and
Theory.BendLiveMachine and retires with them.
