Independent primary-scholar source consumers, 2026-10-03

These files were written against the compiler owner's concrete source grammar.
No parser, typechecker, evaluator or backend success is asserted by authorship.
They deliberately do not import a backend evaluator into their intended meaning.

LazyUnusedArgument.result converges to Nat 7 in the intended non-strict source.
Its unused second argument is fix(identity), which has no weak-head normal form.
A strict argument evaluation CPS machine diverges before entering keep; giving
that machine a resumable clock does not repair the mismatch. A first-order/Data
backend CAN pass by encoding delayed expressions and environments correctly.

LazyUnusedField.result converges to Nat 7. Tying the whole record lazily is not
sufficient if all fields are evaluated when the record is first demanded. The
bad field must remain suspended when good is selected. Requesting bad instead
has no Nat result; resource exhaustion must remain suspension, not zero or a
successful mathematical fixed-point witness.

LazySharedField.result converges to Nat 512 in pure CBN and call-by-need alike.
It also discriminates their cost: call-by-need must evaluate the expensive
field's ONE thunk identity at most once, and force its cached result on the
second selection. Ordinary distinct work(8n) calls are not required to share.
Observe diagnostic counts privately or in a specified public cost semantics;
do not add an effectful counter and silently assume a theorem for the pure core.
A checkpoint after that field has been forced must preserve its cached value
and identity on restore. Serialization success alone is not that theorem.

Primary motivation: LTUO sections 5.3.2.3 (Lazy Y), 5.3.2.5 and 10.3.2
(Suspended Records or Records of Suspensions). These particular programs are
our independent discriminators, not quoted examples or claims made by Faré.
